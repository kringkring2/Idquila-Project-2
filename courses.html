About Kabankalan Catholic College

Education that transforms lives

At Kabankalan Catholic College, students receive a holistic education that balances academic rigor, moral formation, and community service. Our learning community encourages every student to discover their talents and apply them for the common good.

Through dedicated faculty and student-centered programs, KCC prepares learners to meet life's challenges with confidence, integrity, and compassion.
