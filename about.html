About KCC

A learning community formed by faith, committed to excellence, and prepared to serve society.

Our Institution

Education that develops the whole person

Kabankalan Catholic College provides a Christ-centered and student-focused education that balances academic excellence with moral formation. We help learners develop the knowledge, character, and confidence to contribute meaningfully to their communities.

Grounded in the values of faith, excellence, and service, KCC prepares graduates to respond to the needs of society with competence, integrity, and compassion.

Our Compass

Vision & Mission

01

Vision

Kabankalan Catholic College, Inc., as builder of the future, is a Christ-centered learning community of accountable and socially responsible individuals, that leads positive change, responds to and serves the mission of the Diocese, the nation, and the world.

02

Mission

KCC commits to provide quality and inclusive Catholic education, rooted in the Gospel and driven by innovative research and meaningful community engagements, empowering its members to become effective agents of societal transformation.
